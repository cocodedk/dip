Rewards should recognize **remembered, distinct, reviewed material**, while the official mock score stays separate. Proposed XP: 10 for a first correct answer to a verified item, 4 for a correct review on a later day, 0 for repeats on the same day; wrong answers lose no XP and schedule review. Rank uses the number of distinct verified items answered correctly on **two different days**: *Begynder* 0–24, *Opdager* 25–99, *Øver* 100–199, *Kender* 200–299, and *Fordyber* 300+. These thresholds are design defaults, not exam standards.

Small rewards: a chapter badge after at least 80% on a chapter review, a values badge after 5/5 on a values set, and a “three mocks passed” badge only when **both** pass thresholds were met on three full current mocks. Show a weekly completed-review count; do not break a streak or punish a missed day. Ranks and badges are private/local and never imply citizenship eligibility or official certification.

**Why:** Repeated recall over time is the learning behavior supported by [[learning-methods]]; the values badge draws attention to the separate [[pass-rule]].
