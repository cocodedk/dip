description: The two simultaneous thresholds for passing
metadata:

  updated: 2026-09-30
---

Passing requires **at least 36 correct answers out of 45 and at least four correct answers among questions 41–45 on Danish values**. No particular individual values question is compulsory: one mistake in that group is permitted, while two fail the test even if the total is 36 or higher. The three older 40-question tests in the archive required 32 correct and had no separate values section.

**Sources:** [SIRI, Om Indfødsretsprøven](https://danskogproever.dk/borger/indfoedsretsproeve-statsborgerskab/om-indfoedsretsproeven/); [official June 2026 answer key](https://danskogproever.dk/media/uyspi1xi/indfoedsretsproeven-2026-06-retteark.pdf); [official June 2021 test](https://danskogproever.dk/media/jk4inhpt/indfoedsretsproeven-2021-06.pdf).

**Apply:** Always show two scores and the reason for a failed mock; never mark one specific question as an automatic failure. Score historical 40-question tests under their own rule. See [[exam-format]], [[practice-modes]].
