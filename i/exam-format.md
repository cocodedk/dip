The current Indfødsretsprøven is a 45-minute written test of 45 questions, each with two or three choices: 35 based on the official study material, five about recent Danish events, and five about Danish values. The recent-events questions concern matters of substantial media interest in roughly the preceding six months; the values questions can concern free speech, equality, or religion and law. No aids are allowed in the real test.

**Source:** [SIRI, Om Indfødsretsprøven](https://danskogproever.dk/borger/indfoedsretsproeve-statsborgerskab/om-indfoedsretsproeven/).

**Apply:** A current mock must draw 35 + 5 + 5 and run for 45 minutes; answer choices must preserve the official A/B/C format. See [[pass-rule]], [[practice-modes]].
