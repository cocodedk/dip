The currently linked SIRI study material is the **August 2026 edition**, 246 pages, applicable to winter 2026 and summer 2027; SIRI says a replacement is due at the end of August 2027. Its six chapters are Danish history, democracy, economy, Denmark and the wider world, cultural life, and topical reference entries (*Temaopslag*). SIRI also supplies matching audio chapters. This book covers the 35 study-material questions, not the five recent-events or five values questions.

**Sources:** [SIRI preparation page](https://danskogproever.dk/borger/indfoedsretsproeve-statsborgerskab/forberedelse-til-indfoedsretsproeven/); [August 2026 study material PDF](https://danskogproever.dk/media/natdfdrw/laeremateriale-til-indfoedsretsproeven.pdf), title, colophon and contents pages.
