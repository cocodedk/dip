Historical answer keys are authoritative **for their own test dates**, but are not evidence that an answer is correct for the next test. The archive contains names, offices, population figures, laws, prices and news that can change. Recent-events questions are inherently date-specific, and some values questions cite dated external rankings. Also, the August 2026 study edition supersedes the material used for older tests.

**Why:** SIRI identifies recent-events and values questions as outside the study material and warns that unofficial practice sites can be inaccurate or misleading. [SIRI preparation page](https://danskogproever.dk/borger/indfoedsretsproeve-statsborgerskab/forberedelse-til-indfoedsretsproeven/); [SIRI test format](https://danskogproever.dk/borger/indfoedsretsproeve-statsborgerskab/om-indfoedsretsproeven/).

**How to apply:** Keep historical questions unchanged in a clearly dated “Past tests” mode. For the current pool, mark each item `reviewedForEdition`, `reviewedAt`, `sourceSection`, and `validUntil` or `historicalOnly`; verify every answer and option, retire ambiguous items, and refresh recent-events items before each test. A question without a current review must not enter the current mock. See [[question-bank]], [[study-material]].
