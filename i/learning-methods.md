The learning loop should ask for an answer **before** showing it, give the correct answer with a short source-backed explanation, and bring the item back on later days. Research reviews find practice testing and distributed practice useful across many learning settings; the exact reminder intervals below are a product heuristic, not a scientifically mandated schedule. Sources: [Dunlosky et al., review of effective learning techniques](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/journals/pspi/learning-techniques.html); [McDermott, retrieval-practice review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33006925/); [Cepeda et al., spacing synthesis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16719566/).

**Proposed loop:** Start with 10 mixed questions or one chapter; show the answer and explanation after a committed response; put wrong or uncertain items back later in the session and again after roughly 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days if recalled correctly; reset to the next day after a miss. Mix chapters in later reviews, and prioritize the five values questions because their pass rule is separate. During a timed mock, withhold feedback until submission to preserve realistic test conditions.

**Measure:** Count distinct, source-verified items recalled on a later day, review completion and mock performance; avoid treating repeated same-day guesses as mastery. Explanations should link to the official study section or a dated source. See [[practice-modes]], [[rewards-and-ranks]].

Links: [[practice-modes]], [[rewards-and-ranks]].

**Delivered first version:** The app gives the official dated answer and answer-key link after practice, schedules review after 1/3/7/14/30 days of later-day successes and resets misses to tomorrow. Daily practice prioritizes due items; a dedicated Mistakes mode allows another same-session attempt. Ranks require correct recall on two different days, and tests withhold answers until completion. Automatic reinsertion into the same set, uncertainty ratings, chapter mapping and longer explanations are not implemented; the historical answer key is the feedback source.
