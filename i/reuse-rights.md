SIRI publishes the past tests and answer keys for free preparation, but the pages and PDF colophon inspected here did not state a reuse license for republishing all questions in an app. Public availability alone does not establish a redistribution license: [Digitaliseringsstyrelsen's guidance on public-data licenses](https://digst.dk/data/videreanvendelse-af-offentlige-data/licens-og-brugsvilkaar-for-offentlige-data/) explains that publications without a license may still be copyrighted.

**Next action before public release:** Ask SIRI for the reuse terms for verbatim questions and answers, keep source attribution, and use links to the official PDFs or independently written questions if the rights do not allow redistribution. The [local extraction](data/official-exams.json) is a research artifact for building and review. See [[question-bank]], [[app-plan]].
