The implemented flows are **Daily** (due and new archival material/values), **Material**, **Values**, **Mistakes** and **Historical news**, with 10, 20 or 50 items bounded by available distinct concepts. **Tests** contains 13 dated official originals and a mixed 45-question archival test. It uses 35 material, five historical news and five values questions, a 45-minute saved deadline and both current-format scoring thresholds. It does not predict the next exam's current events. Chapter mapping was deferred rather than guessed.

Practice gives the official correct answer and answer-key link after a committed response. Tests allow revisions and reveal answers only after submission or timeout. Unanswered submitted questions count as wrong and can be repeated from Mistakes. A result shows overall and values scores, both requirements, XP and missed answers. The result is practice feedback, never an official citizenship decision.

Sources: [SIRI's exam format](https://danskogproever.dk/borger/indfoedsretsproeve-statsborgerskab/om-indfoedsretsproeven/) and the dated original sources on every [question](data/official-exams.json). Learning decisions follow [[learning-methods]].
